- Is Starship an offer, a curriculum, a brand world, a content engine, a software product, an internal agent system, or a hybrid?
- What should exist first as a concrete artifact?
- Is the primary audience internal collaborators, future clients, or both?

## Launch Context

- Is there a target launch date?
- Is there an existing audience channel this needs to fit into?
- Is there a price point or revenue goal?
- Are there current clients or proof points that should shape the offer?

## Experience

- Is the intended container one-to-one, group, async, retreat-based, AI-supported, or mixed?
- What practices or modalities are non-negotiable?
- What topics should be handled carefully or avoided publicly?

## Success Metrics

- What should a client be able to do after the first 7 days?
- What should be measurably different after the full experience?
- What evidence would make the buyer feel certain this worked?
